# Product Pricing & Subscription Tiers

CloudScale Systems offers three standardized subscription tiers structured to support organizations from early growth through multinational enterprises.

## Tier Comparison Matrix

| Feature / Entitlement | Starter Tier | Growth Tier | Enterprise Tier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Target Audience** | Early stage startups | Mid-market companies | Global Fortune 500 enterprises |
| **Product Nova Support** | Shared cluster | Dedicated namespace | Dedicated VPC / On-Prem |
| **Product Orion Event Ingress** | Up to 10 GB/day | Up to 500 GB/day | Unlimited metered ingress |
| **Product Atlas Governance** | Not included | Standard audit log | Full SOC2/GDPR automation |
| **Product Vega Telemetry** | 100k metrics/sec | 1M metrics/sec | 10M+ metrics/sec |
| **Authentication & SSO** | Email + Password | SAML 2.0 SSO | SAML 2.0, OIDC, OAuth 2.1 PKCE |
| **SLA Availability** | 99.5% uptime | 99.9% uptime | 99.99% uptime with financial credits |
| **API Gateway Ingress Rate** | 100 RPS | 1,000 RPS | 25,000+ RPS |
| **Dedicated Support** | Community forums | Business hours email | 24/7/365 dedicated Technical Account Mgr |

## Enterprise Add-ons
- **Dedicated VPC Peering**: Direct connection into AWS us-east-1 or Azure East US.
- **On-Premise Private Cluster**: Licensed deployment for air-gapped data centers.
- **Custom Policy Rules**: Unlocks arbitrary ABAC rule compilation via **Policy Engine**.
- **Active Enterprise Customers**: **Acme Corp**, **Globex Corp**, and **Initech** are all active subscribers under custom Enterprise Tier contracts.
